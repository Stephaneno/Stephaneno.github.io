* 9/23/2026*

---
**Importance of Sequencing Technology:**

Sequencing technology is a very important part of modern cancer research so we can create models that display how cancer can form and then try to use that in order to prevent or treat cancer, and better understand the genetics, rather than trying to figure out the origin like in the past. By looking at some companies founded in sequencing, we can better understand how grand the contributions of sequencing can be.
 
 --- 
 
**Tempus Ai and their Impact:** 

Tempus, founded by Eric Lefkofsky, was created with the intention to develop an ai that could analyze a sample of cancerous DNA to figure out the sequencing and administer treatment and doses tailored specifically to that patients sequencing. Starting with about 600 million dollars of public donations and some undisclosed seed funds from larger companies, has now turned into a revenue of 1.27 billion dollars. 

**Tempus moving Forward:**

While their original focus was to create a model that could understand sequencing to treat and prevent cancer, it is continuing to evolve to help doctors identify and prescribe other conditions based on reading DNA samples. Some examples include, radiology analysis, insights for conditions such as depression, anxiety, and ADHD, detecting infections, and going forward hopefully it can identify much more. As it continues to grow and develop its database, the more capabilities it will gain to help assist in all areas in the medical field to hopefully make the world a better place with easier determination of illness.
