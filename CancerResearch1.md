*2026-09-02*

**About Chondrosarcoma Cancer:**

Stages/subtypes, identification, what it does, science behind the cancer.

**Causes:** 

The most common cause for Chondrosarcoma is genetic mutations at birth, most notibly:

Li Fraumeni syndrome, a rare genetic disorder that makes is where you are a lot more likely to develope certain types of cancers, not limited to Chondrosarcoma, but it does make it a lot more likely to develope it.

Maffucci syndrome and Ollier’s disease, which is more restricted to Chondrosarcoma, only really causing cancer in cartilage, bone, and skin, two of the three places that Chondrosarcoma can occur.

Hereditary multiple osteochondromas, a condition that causes cancer on your bones and skips the process of growing on cartilage like regular Chondrosarcoma.

While these are the genetic conditions, just like most other cancers, genetic mutations can cause this cancer to occur in any stage of life even without the predisposition of the genetic disorders. Currently though, it is unknown what outside factors may make this mutation more likely to occur so at the moment it is random.

**Frequencies:**

The frequency of this type of cancer is a bit rare, being only 1 out of every 200,000, however this cancer can become much more common later in life or depending on if you have one of the genetic disorders that affect common areas of Chondrosarcoma. Mesenchymal chondrosarcoma is one specific instance that only tends to appear around the age of 19 to 30.

**Common Treatment:**

The most common treatment, depending on the stage is either surgery or chemotherapy
