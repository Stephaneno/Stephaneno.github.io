# Chondrosarcoma Cancer Information: 

*2026-09-02*

---
**About Chondrosarcoma Cancer:**


This is a cancer that starts as a mutation in cartilaginous cells that starts growing cancer that can spread to bones close by, this cartilidge can be in between almost any bone joint, but is most common in-between pelvis joints, thigh joints, ribs, and upper arms. (Can also form in jaw and spinal chord in rare instances) 

It can be harder to identify because usually you cannot visibly see or feel the cancer, but once you start experiencing symptoms of nausea, weight loss, pain, or weakness, your provider can do an x-ray, PET scan, or bone scan to help identify it.

In the scenario a bump does occur you can also have a biopsy done.

**Some stages of Chondrosarcoma are:**


Low-grade (grade I):  Starts as cartilaginous cancer and not very likely to start spreading until it reaches the bone.


Intermediate grade (grade II): Starts spreading faster to bone.


High-grade (grade III): Expansive, fast spread to bone.


---


**Causes:** 



The most common cause for Chondrosarcoma is genetic mutations at birth, most notibly:


Li Fraumeni syndrome, a rare genetic disorder that makes is where you are a lot more likely to develope certain types of cancers, not limited to Chondrosarcoma, but it does make it a lot more likely to develope it.


Maffucci syndrome and Ollier’s disease, which is more restricted to Chondrosarcoma, only really causing cancer in cartilage, bone, and skin, two of the three 
places that Chondrosarcoma can occur.


Hereditary multiple osteochondromas, a condition that causes cancer on your bones and skips the process of growing on cartilage like regular Chondrosarcoma.


While these are the genetic conditions, just like most other cancers, genetic mutations can cause this cancer to occur in any stage of life even without the predisposition of the genetic disorders. Currently though, it is unknown what outside factors may make this mutation more likely to occur so at the moment it is random.

---


**Frequencies:**


The frequency of this type of cancer is a bit rare, being only 1 out of every 200,000, however this cancer can become much more common later in life or depending on if you have one of the genetic disorders that affect common areas of Chondrosarcoma. Mesenchymal chondrosarcoma is one specific instance that only tends to appear around the age of 19 to 30.

---

**Common Treatment:**



The most common treatment, depending on the stage is either surgery or chemotherapy. 


Chemotherapy is only really used after surgery or for small amounts of the cancer. However, once it reaches the bone, most common treatment is either bone grafting (cutting the cancer off the bone and replacing the cut with different bone), or amputation if it is only on one limb.

---


**Life Expectancies and Mortality Rates:**


If found in early stages, the 5 year survival rate is 91%


If found while starting to spread to bones, the 5 year survival rate is 71%


If it manages to spread to bones in different parts of your body, the 5 year survival rate is 28%
